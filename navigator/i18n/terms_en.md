# Terms of use and privacy policy

**Version 1.0 · 27 October 2026**

## 1. What the program is
Decarbonisation Navigator is developed as part of an undergraduate thesis at the University of West Attica. It evaluates ship decarbonisation investments under the IMO Net-Zero Framework, the EU ETS and FuelEU Maritime. It is under development.

## 2. Not professional advice
Results are model estimates with assumptions and uncertainty. They are not investment, financial, legal or technical advice. Decisions made with the program are the user's responsibility. Any investment needs review by qualified experts.

## 3. Data, regulations and sources
- Ship data come from EMSA's public EU MRV releases.
- Methods and parameters come from published sources, cited in the program and in the report.
- Regulations change, for example with the IMO vote on the Net-Zero Framework and the EU ETS review.
- Values that have not been confirmed are marked "to verify".

## 4. Privacy: everything stays on your computer
- Accounts, projects and preferences are stored only locally, in the program's data folder.
- The program sends no data anywhere and collects no usage analytics.
- Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as cryptographic hashes (scrypt), never as text.
- In guest mode, projects are deleted when a new guest session starts.

## 5. Accounts and recovery
- The recovery code is shown once, at sign-up, and is the only way to set a new password.
- Keep it somewhere safe. If both the password and the recovery code are lost, the account cannot be recovered, but the project files remain in the data folder.
- Local accounts protect against casual access on a shared computer. They do not replace the operating system's security.
